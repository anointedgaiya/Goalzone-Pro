☁️ Multi-device tracking

The current dashboard is local. For totals across everyone using your published website, GoalZone needs a shared analytics service or database rather than browser-only storage.

For production, use an authenticated server/database for owner reports and keep phone numbers out of analytics unless you have a clear privacy basis for collecting them.
